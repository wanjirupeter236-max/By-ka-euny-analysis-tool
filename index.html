How the percentages work: the tool counts the digits received from the live tick stream and calculates the percentage from that sample. “Over N” means digit > N and “Under N” means digit < N. Even means 0,2,4,6,8 and Odd means 1,3,5,7,9. The “Most Powerful Digit” is only the most frequent digit in the current sample; it is not a guarantee of the next tick or of a winning trade.
